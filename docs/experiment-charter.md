# Stoneham commercial world-model experiment

## Hypothesis
Statewide public address/parcel data can anchor a commercial graph; local public business data can supply occupancy evidence. Test whether construction is accurate, reproducible and transferable, rather than assuming a complete commercial census.

## Scope
Stoneham first, downtown/Main Street and other commercial clusters. Melrose is a later generalization test. Keep a 16-case seed for smoke testing and independently audit at least 50 diverse business locations for final evaluation, including multi-tenant sites, DBAs, offices and businesses with limited digital presence. Use independently sourced evidence and freeze reference labels before running evaluation. Report sampling bias.

## Acceptance
Gate A: address/parcel precision >= 95%, recall >= 90% each; provenance 100%.
Gate B: core-chain precision >= 90%, recall >= 85%, false-positive merge rate < 2%, provenance 100%. Website resolution target 85%; legal entity target 70% where auditable, both secondary. Licenses/permits are optional enrichment.
Final: GO only if gates pass with credible evidence and reasonable maintenance; CONDITIONAL for major source/coverage limitations; NO-GO for inadequate accuracy. Melrose transfer is required before asserting statewide scalability.

## Outputs
Source manifest and vintages, normalized data contracts, test results, benchmark methodology and results, unresolved/conflict diagnostics, source coverage, acquisition costs and final decision. No inference of unmet demand from business counts alone; that requires additional demand evidence.
